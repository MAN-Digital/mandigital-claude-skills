## The ten observable v1 → v2 differences

1. **Title register.** "HubSpot Price Books: A Simple Guide" — not "…, Deconstructed" or
   "… Breakdown."
2. **Cold open = a two-line concrete scenario with real numbers.** "Two sales reps sell the
   same service. One quotes five thousand euros. The other quotes four thousand." No
   "Welcome back," no company introduction, no feature announcement (the title and
   thumbnail already announce the feature).
3. **First-person roadmap in one or two lines.** "I will explain price books. Then we will
   build and test one." Not a bulleted "we cover four things" agenda.
4. **Max two lines per paragraph, blank line between every chunk.** The layout is the
   pacing.
5. **Every abstract term gets an immediate inline example.** "For example: 'Direct - UK -
   GBP' and 'Partners - Germany - EUR.'" — on nearly every concept.
6. **Structural self-answered questions as teaching devices.** "First, what is a tax rate
   library? It is one list of the taxes your team uses." (Audience-directed hooks remain
   banned.)
7. **Plan/permission facts stated plainly.** Hub tier, seats, Super Admin, beta status —
   near the start of the walkthrough.
8. **Warnings are plain do/don't sentences, not aphorisms.** "Do not use an unclear field
   just because it already exists." — not "Your margin melts, and your forecast follows."
9. **Numbered pre-flight checklist near the end.** "Before you finish, check five things.
   One: … Two: …" — one item per line, spoken ordinals.
10. **Soft closing CTA only.** "If your HubSpot pricing is difficult to control, send us the
    setup. We will show you where it breaks." No "Elite HubSpot partner" self-promo block,
    no "link in the description" hard sell.
